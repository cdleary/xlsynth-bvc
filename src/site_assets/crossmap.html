What this comparison isolates

All six points start from the same XLS IR function. The native path lowers IR directly to a g8r AIG. The Yosys path uses XLS combinational Verilog, Yosys lowering, and ABC Boolean optimization to produce a retained AIG. Both native and Yosys AIGs are then mapped by g8r; the Yosys AIG is also mapped by ABC.

g8r has separate area and delay strategies. ABC area-only uses map -a; its default map is timing-oriented and includes area recovery. These runs set no explicit ABC delay target.

The new Yosys-AIG → g8r netlists are converted back to AIGs and formally checked against their retained source AIG. Original native and ABC points are baseline measurements; the supplement does not add a new proof claim to them.

A measured endpoint is nondominated if no other available endpoint has both no larger area and no larger delay, with at least one strict improvement. Values within a relative or absolute tolerance of 10⁻⁹ are treated as ties. The two strategies sample possible tradeoffs and do not establish a continuous Pareto frontier.
